Questions

Good to know

Is the farmer app available now?

Not yet. We are building it now. Join the early list and you will be among the first to use it.

Which languages does Sheetkosh support?

The Cold Storage ERP works in Hindi and English today. More regional languages are coming soon.

Does the ERP need internet?

No. It runs on your office PC and keeps working without internet. Several office PCs can work together.

Is my data safe?

Your records stay on your own office PC. When the farmer app arrives, only what each farmer needs to see will be shared with them.

Can I move from my old cold storage software?

Yes. Talk to us during the demo and we will look at your current records with you.

Is it only for potato?

No. Sheetkosh is built for storage facilities and farmers across crops, and we are growing beyond cold storage.
